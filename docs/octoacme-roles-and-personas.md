# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Engineering Managers

### Role Summary
Engineering Managers support delivery health, team capacity, and technical execution. They work closely with Project Managers and Product Managers to keep engineering work aligned with priorities, dependencies, and quality expectations.

### Responsibilities
- Manage delivery planning for engineering squads and team capacity
- Support hiring, onboarding, and team capability development
- Coordinate technical prioritization and trade-offs with product and project leadership
- Help remove blockers, resolve dependency conflicts, and keep delivery predictable
- Mentor engineers and support healthy engineering practices

### Goals
- Maintain sustainable delivery velocity
- Improve technical execution quality and team health
- Reduce avoidable bottlenecks and cross-team coordination friction

### Typical Communication
- Engineering planning and staffing discussions
- Weekly delivery health updates with PM and Product Lead
- Technical risk reviews and cross-team dependency tracking

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers on workload, mentorship, and technical quality
- Partners with Project Managers on scope, timelines, and resource constraints
- Aligns with Product Managers on roadmap trade-offs and delivery impact

---

## QA Leads / Test Managers

### Role Summary
QA Leads define the quality strategy and validation approach for a project. They ensure that releases meet acceptance criteria, user expectations, and operational readiness standards.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy, coverage goals, and release readiness criteria
- Validate acceptance criteria and coordinate manual and automated testing
- Monitor regressions and quality trends across milestones
- Partner with developers on defect triage and prevention strategies
- Support release sign-off and escalation when quality risks remain open

### Goals
- Reduce defects reaching production
- Improve confidence in release quality and customer experience
- Align testing effort to business risk and delivery priorities

### Typical Communication
- QA planning and acceptance review meetings
- Defect triage and release readiness check-ins
- Cross-functional status reports during milestones and releases

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Works closely with Developers to validate implementation quality and bugs
- Coordinates with Project Managers on testing milestones and release gates
- Supports Product Managers by ensuring features meet user requirements and acceptance criteria

---

## Security / Compliance Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance Partners ensure projects meet required controls, risk standards, and safe delivery practices. They help teams assess security, privacy, and regulatory implications before release.

### Responsibilities
- Review technical designs, data handling, and system risks
- Validate that security and compliance requirements are included in scope and delivery plans
- Partner with engineering and stakeholders on remediation and control implementation
- Support incident response readiness and security review gates
- Help document risk exposure, mitigations, and approval requirements

### Goals
- Protect customer trust and business continuity
- Reduce security and compliance risk in delivery and operations
- Ensure compliance requirements are considered early, not late

### Typical Communication
- Design review and architecture checkpoints
- Security risk assessments and remediation planning
- Escalation updates during high-risk or incident-related work

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Advises Developers on secure architecture and safe implementation patterns
- Coordinates with Project Managers on risk tracking, escalation, and timeline impact
- Aligns with Product Managers and stakeholders on acceptable risk levels and release decisions

---

## Technical Program Managers

### Role Summary
Technical Program Managers coordinate complex cross-team initiatives and dependencies. They help connect planning, delivery, and communication across multiple workstreams or platforms.

### Responsibilities
- Manage program-level milestones, dependencies, and risk across teams
- Align stakeholders on sequencing, trade-offs, and shared commitments
- Track inter-team dependencies and drive resolution of delivery blockers
- Consolidate status reporting for portfolio or multi-team work
- Support planning for large initiatives that span multiple squads or systems

### Goals
- Improve cross-team coordination and planning accuracy
- Reduce duplication, missed handoffs, and dependency surprises
- Keep multi-team initiatives moving toward measurable outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Cross-functional program reviews and dependency tracking
- Weekly updates with PMs, engineering leads, and stakeholders
- Leadership communication for progress, risks, and strategic decisions

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Partners with Project Managers on planning and execution rhythm
- Works with Engineering Managers to manage workload and sequencing
- Supports Product Managers in translating roadmap priorities into coordinated delivery plans

---

## Customer Success / Support Partners

### Role Summary
Customer Success and Support Partners connect delivery work to real user needs, operational realities, and adoption outcomes. They make sure teams understand the customer impact of changes and support readiness.

### Responsibilities
- Capture customer feedback, support trends, and adoption issues
- Identify user pain points and operational risks tied to product changes
- Support launch readiness, communication, and post-release monitoring
- Help prioritize fixes that protect customer experience and retention
- Share customer insights with product and delivery teams

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and product adoption
- Reduce avoidable support escalations after release
- Make customer impact visible in planning and decision-making

### Typical Communication
- Customer feedback loops and issue triage updates
- Launch readiness and post-release review sessions
- Cross-functional conversations on user impact and risk

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Feeds real-world user insights to Product Managers and Developers
- Coordinates with Project Managers on rollout readiness and communication needs
- Helps QA and release teams prioritize where customer-facing quality risks are highest

---

## Data / Analytics Partners

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Partners provide evidence, dashboards, and measurement support so teams can make decisions based on usage, performance, and outcomes rather than assumptions.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, instrumentation plans, and reporting needs
- Create or maintain dashboards for product, delivery, and operational health
- Validate whether a feature or release is delivering expected value
- Support experimentation, trend analysis, and risk monitoring
- Help teams interpret performance data and identify follow-up actions

### Goals
- Improve data-informed decision making
- Increase visibility into outcomes, quality, and usage trends
- Ensure teams have the information needed to iterate confidently

### Typical Communication
- Metric definition and dashboard review meetings
- Weekly or milestone-based reporting to PM and product stakeholders
- Analysis of release performance and adoption signals

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Helps Product Managers measure whether goals and outcomes are being met
- Provides evidence for Developers and QA to validate quality and user impact
- Supports Project Managers and PMs in tracking milestones, risks, and release readiness with measurable data

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- These additional roles help teams clarify accountability, decision rights, and coordination as projects become more complex.
